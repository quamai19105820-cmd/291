Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

1. Data Collection

AO V Trade does not collect any personal identifiable information (PII). We do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device permissions unless explicitly required for core functionality and fully disclosed in system settings.

  • No personal information is gathered.
  • No usage analytics or tracking data is captured.
  • No ad identifiers are accessed.

2. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Because no data is collected, no data is shared, sold, or disclosed to third parties, advertisers, or affiliate partners. The app does not embed third-party trackers or analytics SDKs.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

The app does not use cookies. Any local storage used is strictly limited to non-identifiable application settings (e.g., dark mode preference) and can be cleared at any time by clearing app data in your device settings.

4. Security

While we do not collect data, we ensure that any internal state data remains encrypted at rest where supported by the operating system. No data is transmitted over the network, ensuring maximum security through the absence of network exposure.

5. Children's Privacy

This application is not intended for children under 13. Because no personal data is collected, it does not process any information from children in violation of COPPA or GDPR-K requirements.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold. Since we hold no personal data, this right is inherently satisfied. If you have questions, you can contact the developer via the official support channels.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, this policy will be updated with a new "Last Updated" date. Since we currently collect no data, changes are unlikely but will be communicated through app updates.

8. Contact

For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact the developer of AO V Trade.
